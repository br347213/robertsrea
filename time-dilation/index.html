02 · Common questions

The parts that usually feel weird.

Wouldn’t each person say the other one is moving?

During steady relative motion, yes. The returning traveler changes inertial frames during the turnaround, so the complete trip is not symmetric. When they reunite, the elapsed times can differ.

Does the effect stop when the spaceship stops?

The extra velocity-based time dilation stops when the high relative speed stops. The age difference already accumulated remains.

Is this just because light takes time to reach us?

No. Signal delay is separate. Time dilation remains after accounting for light-travel time.

Optional math

The equation behind the slider

traveler time = Earth time × √(1 − v²/c²)

Here, v is the traveler’s speed and c is the speed of light.

Interactive intuition first. Equation second.
